The new Option Group, `slab-option-group`, is a named `[role=group]` of Options for Listbox, Select and Combobox. Its Options are still the Listbox's, Select's or Combobox's, so the arrow keys, typeahead and selection work across groups as across the rest. Combobox hides an Option Group while its filter hides every Option in it.
