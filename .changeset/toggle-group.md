Adds Toggle Group, `slab-toggle-group`, and its `slab-toggle-group-button`: a row of toggle buttons that Tab reaches once and the arrow keys move between. One button is pressed at a time, or several with `multiple`. Set `pressed` on a button that starts out pressed; the group handles `aria-pressed`. `value` and `values` read and set which are pressed.
