Left and Right now follow the reading direction in Tabs, Toolbar, Toggle Group, Radio Group, Menubar, Tree, Calendar, Color Picker's swatches and Combobox's tags. In a right-to-left page, Left moves to the next item, as it does for native radios, Tree expands with Left and collapses with Right, Calendar moves forward a day with Left, and Combobox moves from the start of its input to its tags with Right.
