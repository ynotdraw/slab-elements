Menu has submenus. Put a `slab-menu` inside another with a `slab-menu-item` as its anchor, and it opens beside that Menu Item. The Right arrow, Enter, Space, a click, or resting the pointer on the Menu Item for a moment opens it. Left and Escape close it and return focus to its Menu Item, and a click on any Menu Item in it closes every Menu. In a right-to-left page, Left and Right swap.
